04A · ARPA-H Project

Surgical Scene Intelligence

We are developing a multi-camera perception stack for operating-room assistance. RGB and depth observations support surgical-tool recognition and tracking, 3D position and constrained pose estimation, hand-pose understanding, and temporally consistent bleeding-region tracking. These signals can be aligned in a shared coordinate system for downstream humanoid planning and assistance.

  • Few-shot tool annotation
  • Tool detection and tracking
  • RGB-D position and pose
  • 3D hand understanding
  • Bleeding-region tracking
  • Multi-camera alignment
04B · Efficient Embodied AI

Object-Centric Spatial Token Pruning

Vision-language-action models process many visual patches that are not equally useful for the current manipulation step. We train a lightweight spatial scorer to retain patches around the robot hand, task objects, goals, and the paths between them. A motion-aware gate restores full visual context near fine-grained interactions, while the underlying policy remains frozen.

  • Task-aware visual selection
  • Agent and wrist views
  • Object-centric spatial cues
  • Motion-aware token gating
  • Frozen-policy integration
