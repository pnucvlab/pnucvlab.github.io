PNU CVLab · Research

Visual intelligence for open-world understanding and interaction.

We study how machines can recognize, reconstruct, and act in complex visual environments. Our work spans open-ended vision-language recognition, 3D scene understanding, Gaussian-based scene reconstruction and relighting, and embodied perception for robotic and surgical systems.

Training-free

Recognizing Any Region with Frozen VLMs

Given an image and region mask, Retrieve–Augment–Generate recognizes regions without predefined class lists or additional training, using internal evidence from frozen VLMs.

  • Augment with grounded candidate labels.

02

3D Scene Understanding

Understanding 3D environments beyond object categories.

Research Theme

Toward richer semantic understanding of the 3D world.

3D scene understanding extends beyond assigning categories to objects by capturing richer semantics such as properties, affordances, and spatial relationships. This enables machines to better understand and interact with complex 3D environments, supporting applications such as robotics and autonomous systems.

  • Open-Vocabulary 3D Scene Understanding
  • Open-Ended 3D Instance Segmentation
  • Online Scene Understanding

03

3DGS / 2DGS Reconstruction & Relighting

Scene reconstruction and relighting with Gaussian splatting.

Research Theme

Reconstructing surfaces. Exploring light.

We study how images, depth estimation, normal estimation, and laser-scanned geometry can work together to reconstruct outdoor scenes and support changes in illumination.

  • Combine visual and geometric cues for reconstruction.
  • Refine surfaces with depth and normal supervision.
  • Explore relighting and geometry-induced shadows.
