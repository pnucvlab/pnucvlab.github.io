PNU CVLab · Research

Visual intelligence for open-world understanding and interaction.

We study how machines can recognize, reconstruct, manipulate, and act in complex visual environments. Our work spans open-ended vision-language recognition, 3D scene understanding, Gaussian-based scene manipulation, and embodied perception for robotic and surgical systems.

Open-Ended Recognition

Training-free

Recognizing Any Region with Frozen VLMs

Given an image and region mask, Retrieve–Augment–Generate recognizes regions without predefined class lists or additional training, using internal evidence from frozen VLMs.

  • Augment with grounded candidate labels.

3D Recognition

Open-world semantic understanding across geometry, views, and language.

Research Theme

Recognizing objects and regions directly in 3D scenes.

We investigate 3D representations that connect geometry with semantic evidence from modern vision and vision-language models, with an emphasis on generalization beyond fixed training vocabularies.
